About me

I'm an electrical engineering student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison with a focus on biomedical devices. I like problems where careful measurement and modeling change how a device is built or used.

In high school and college I've worked on deep brain stimulation safety at Northwestern's Feinberg School of Medicine, burn-repair biomaterials at Westlake University in China, and drug discovery for a neglected tropical disease. Today I'm a quality engineering co-op at GE HealthCare and a data analyst at the Wisconsin Center for Education Research.

I also led the Society of Women Engineers chapter at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy, running virtual engineering programs for 120+ students in Nigeria.
